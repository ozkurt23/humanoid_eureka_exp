\paragraph{Conditional stance closure.}
We repeated all 15 schedules with the lateral-first restart disabled in both the live controller and its forward rollouts. Enabled and disabled controllers succeeded in 12/15 and 11/15 episodes, respectively. Eleven pairs succeeded in both conditions, one succeeded only with closure enabled, and three failed in both. Both conditions succeeded on the same five of six held-out schedules. Eleven pairs had bit-identical complete traces, and episode duration was identical within every pair.

Schedule 714 explains the single changed classification. The two logged trajectories first diverged at 44.406~s. At the fifth push (115~N), ankle separation was 48.7~cm with closure enabled and 78.9~cm with it disabled. The enabled controller placed a lifted foot 19.2~cm downwind. With closure disabled the qualified lifted foot landed 10.7~cm upwind. Both episodes lasted 55~s, traveled 1.999~m forward and met the bilateral fingertip criteria. The evidence supports a step-placement effect in this historical case. The held-out schedules show no observed gain in joint task success.
